For customers

How an order works

  1. You request a booking. This is a request, not a purchase, and nothing is charged.
  2. FAZN confirms the product is available, and approves your request.
  3. You give your delivery details, and FAZN sets the delivery fee. You see the full total, including delivery, before you pay.
  4. You pay the total by bank transfer to the account shown in the app, using your payment reference, within the payment window shown.
  5. FAZN checks the transfer against the real bank record and then confirms your order. Telling us you have paid is a notice to us, not proof of payment. An order becomes active only when we confirm it.
  6. We deliver, and you confirm when it arrives.

Prices, payments and changes

  • Prices are in naira and can change. The price you are shown when you request a booking is checked again before you pay, and you will be told if it has moved.
  • Orders that are not approved, quoted or paid in time expire automatically.
  • If you paid after the window ended, tell us. We will check, and flag it for review rather than ignore it.

Problems and refunds

If something is wrong with an order, report it from the order in the app. If an order cannot be fulfilled, or we agree a refund is due, we return the amount you paid by bank transfer and tell you in the app.

Delete your account

For the FAZN app and the FAZN Vendor app.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password, and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out everywhere.

If you can't sign in

Email us from the address on your account, or message us on WhatsApp, and say you want your account deleted. We may ask a question to check it is really you.

fazndev@gmail.com
WhatsApp: +234 905 863 7198

What is erased

  • Your name, email address and password.
  • Customers: your phone number, saved address, landmark, delivery notes and payment name.
  • Vendors: your product prices and stock, so customers are no longer matched to you.
  • Your notification history and the phones signed in to your account.

What we keep, and why

  • Past orders and payments, because they are financial records we must keep. They no longer carry your name or email.
  • Your chat messages with our team, kept in the same anonymous way for our records.

Before you delete

  • Customers: an order that is still in progress has to finish first (delivered, cancelled, declined, expired or refunded). The app will tell you if one is blocking.
  • Deleting is permanent. You can sign up again later with the same email, but your old account and its history will not come back.
  • Vendors who only want a break can switch products to Unavailable instead of deleting.

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026. This policy covers the FAZN app (customers) and the FAZN Vendor app (sellers), and the service behind them.

We keep this short and plain. We collect what we need to run the service and nothing for advertising. We do not sell your information.

What we collect

Everyone

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored only in scrambled, one-way form, so nobody can read it), and your name if you give one.
  • Messages: the chats you have with the FAZN team, including pictures, voice notes and documents you send or receive, and when messages were delivered and read.
  • Alerts: your notification history, and an address for your phone so we can send you alerts (a push token).
  • Security records: sign-in attempts and technical details such as your IP address, kept to stop abuse and protect accounts.

Customers

  • Your phone number, delivery address, nearest landmark, delivery notes, and the state and area you want delivery in.
  • Your orders: what you booked, quantities, prices, delivery fee, status and dates.
  • Payment details for transfers you make to us: the amount, the reference, the name on the bank account you sent from, the time you reported it, and the receipt screenshot if you attach one (we also keep a fingerprint of the image so the same receipt can't be used twice). We never see or store your card or bank login details.
  • Problems you report about an order, and our replies.

Vendors

  • The products you list, your prices and stock, and the state and area you sell from.

The apps do not use advertising or tracking tools, and do not read your contacts or location. The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to send a picture or a receipt.

Why we use it

  • To run your account, take and deliver orders, and confirm payments.
  • To let you and the FAZN team talk, and to alert you when something changes.
  • To keep the service safe, prevent fraud and misuse, and fix problems.
  • To keep records we are required to keep for accounting and the law.

Who can see it

  • FAZN staff see what they need to run orders, payments, support and the marketplace. Customers and vendors never see each other's details.
  • People who help us fulfil an order receive only what is needed, such as the delivery address.
  • Service providers that run the service for us: MongoDB (database), Railway (servers), Cloudinary (storing pictures and files), and Expo together with Apple and Google (delivering phone alerts). They process data on our behalf and only for that purpose.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Your account details, while your account exists.
  • Notification history for about 90 days.
  • Website visitor statistics (not collected by the apps) for about 180 days.
  • Orders and payments are financial records, so we keep them after an account is deleted, without your name or email. Chat messages are kept the same way.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account any time in the app: Account, then Delete account. See how deletion works.
  • See, correct or export the information we hold about you by contacting us. You can change your saved details (address, phone, prices) in the app yourself.
  • Turn alerts off in your phone's Settings at any time.
  • You may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you think we have mishandled your information. We try to handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted. Passwords are stored in one-way form. Access to your information is limited to the staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

Children

FAZN is not for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.

Common questions

Where is my order?

Open it in the app (Orders tab). It shows exactly which step it is on, and you get an alert whenever it moves. If something looks stuck, report a problem from the order or message us.

I paid, but it still says waiting.

After you tap "I've sent the money", we check the transfer against the bank record before confirming. This is usually quick. If it takes longer than you expect, contact us with your payment reference.

I'm not getting alerts.

Open the app, go to Account, and check Phone alerts. If it says it's off, tap it. If you refused earlier, turn notifications on for FAZN in your phone's Settings.

I forgot my password.

Email or message us from the address on your account and we will help you back in.
